Before you deposit

plain-language risk disclosure
  • ▸Forward-priced exits (Mode F). While a rebalance is pending, an exit settles at the price after it executes, not the price you see now, and cannot be cancelled.
  • ▸Observation-window skip is permanent. Opting to skip is once per agent per vault and cannot be undone.
  • ▸Oracle halts freeze the vault. If price sources disagree, deposits and exits are held until the breaker clears.
  • ▸Stacked fees in sub-vaults. Performance fees compound across levels; a Level-2 vault charges ~19% effective, not 10%.
