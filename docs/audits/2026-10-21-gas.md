# Gas baseline for the v2 contracts, 2026-10-21

Report only. No contract source was changed to produce it. Every optimization below is a proposal
for a separate, reviewed contract PR.

### What the numbers are, and are not

- **Execution gas of the call**, as forge reports it. The tables below add the 21,000 intrinsic
  transaction cost. Calldata is not added: for every path except a large rebalance payload it is
  under 1,000 gas.
- **Median and max over the whole test suite**, not a single scripted scenario. A median mixes
  the paths a function can take (for example `deposit` covers both the first time pending path and
  the immediate mint path), and the max is the heaviest case any test exercised, often a sub-vault
  look-through or a multi-asset basket.
- **Test fixtures, not mainnet tokens.** Tokens are `MockERC20` and most price feeds are mocks. Real
  USDC is a proxy with a blacklist check, and a real Chainlink feed is a proxy in front of an
  aggregator, so each real transfer or price read costs roughly 2,600 to 5,000 more than measured
  here. Treat these as a floor.
- **The suite wires a `SubVaultRegistry` into most vaults.** A launch factory with sub-vaults off
  (`allowSubVaults` false, `subVaultRegistry` zero) skips one external registry read per member
  action, about 5,000 gas lower than measured on deposit, activate and exit paths.

## Ranked optimizations

Savings are estimates from EIP-2929 and EIP-2200 opcode prices (cold SLOAD 2,100; fresh slot
SSTORE 22,100 cold; dirty to dirty SSTORE 2,900 warm or 5,000 cold; cold account access 2,600),
applied to the code as it stands at `c3277fc`. Each needs to be measured in its own PR with
`forge snapshot --diff` before anyone relies on the figure. Ranking is by gas saved across a
typical member lifecycle, weighed against how much protocol behaviour the change touches.

### 1. Stop writing the holder count checkpoint when it has not changed (or merge it with the total)

- **Where:** `VaultCore._snapshot` (`VaultCore.sol:537`) pushes three `Checkpoints.History` entries
  on every member mutation: the member's eligible stake, the total eligible stake, and the holder
  count. `Checkpoints.push` (`lib/Checkpoints.sol:20`) appends a new array element whenever the
  timestamp differs from the last one, even when the value is identical.
- **Cost today:** each push in a new second is about 29,000 gas (two cold SLOADs for length and last
  element, a dirty length SSTORE, a fresh element SSTORE). Three per mutation is about 87,000, a
  large share of every `deposit`, `activate`, `skipWindow`, `requestExit` and `settleQueuedExit`.
- **Change:** skip the push when the value equals the latest one (`getAt` returns the same answer
  for every timestamp, so readers cannot tell), or store total eligible stake and holder count in
  one packed checkpoint (`uint64 ts`, `uint32 holders`, `uint160 total`).
- **Estimated saving:** about 25,000 per member action whose holder count does not change (repeat
  deposits, partial exits, queueing) with the skip; about 29,000 on every member action with the
  merge.
- **Risk:** low to medium. Governance reads both values through `pastTotalVotingEligibleShares`
  and `pastHolderCount`; the views can stay as they are. The merge narrows total stake from
  `uint192` to `uint160`, which needs a bound argument.

### 2. Compute NAV once per deposit

- **Where:** `VaultCore._deposit` (`VaultCore.sol:435`) calls `navWad()` unconditionally, even when
  `capacityCapUsdc` is zero and the value is unused. On the immediate path `_mintShares`
  (`VaultCore.sol:507`) calls `navWad()` again. Internal accounting does not move between the two
  calls, so the second result is always identical to the first.
- **Cost today:** one `navWad` is a price read per basket asset (the report's median for
  `ChainlinkOracle.priceWad` is 15,091 and its max 25,056, before real feed proxy costs) plus any
  child look-through.
- **Change:** pass the first NAV into `_mintShares`; skip the call entirely when the cap is zero and
  the deposit goes to the pending path.
- **Estimated saving:** for a three asset basket, roughly 10,000 to 20,000 on an immediate mint
  (second call is warm) and 45,000 to 75,000 on an uncapped first time deposit (whole cold NAV).
- **Risk:** the dedupe on the immediate path is a pure refactor. **Skipping NAV on the pending path
  is a behaviour change, not only a gas change:** today an uncapped vault's pending deposit reverts
  while the oracle breaker is tripped, because `navWad` reverts (the K-4 freeze in the NatSpec of
  `navWad`, `VaultCore.sol:327`). Skipping it would let pending deposits land during a freeze. That
  is a design decision for the owner, not an optimization to take silently.

### 3. Collapse per-voter reveal flags in `Governance`

- **Where:** `revealVote` (`Governance.sol:409`) writes two fresh slots per voter, `revealedOf` and
  `revealedSupportOf`, then updates `forWeight` or `againstWeight`, `revealedWeight` and
  `revealedVoterCount` in three separate proposal slots.
- **Change:** one `uint8` flags mapping per (proposal, voter) holding revealed, support and default
  applied bits; or, cheaper still, overwrite `commitOf` with a two-value sentinel on reveal. Pack
  `revealedWeight` with `revealedVoterCount` in one slot.
- **Estimated saving:** about 22,000 per reveal with a flags mapping, about 41,000 with the
  `commitOf` sentinel, plus about 5,000 from the weight packing. Roughly 19% to 31% of `revealVote`,
  paid by every voter on every proposal. `revealDelegated` reads the delegate's two flags and saves
  one cold read (about 2,100); `applyStandingDefault` writes only `defaultApplied` and is unchanged.
- **Risk:** low. Contained to `Governance` storage. The sentinel variant changes the meaning of a
  public getter, so the flags mapping is the cleaner first step.

### 4. Pack the `Proposal` struct

- **Where:** `Governance.Proposal` (`Governance.sol:129`). `propose` writes seven fresh slots:
  vault and type; proposer and `createdAt`; the four `uint64` deadlines; `status` alone in its own
  slot; `actionHash`; `snapshotTotal`; `memberCount`.
- **Change:** put `status` beside `vault` and `ptype`; carry `memberCount` and `revealedVoterCount`
  as `uint32`; carry the stake weights as `uint128` pairs. Five slots at creation instead of seven.
- **Estimated saving:** about 44,000 per `propose` (about 19%), and a few thousand on `finalize`
  and `execute`, which read the status and deadlines.
- **Risk:** low to medium. Needs a bound argument for `uint128` weights (the checkpoints already cap
  stake at `uint192`), and the public `proposals` getter changes shape, which touches the backend's
  ABI decoding.

### 5. Pack per-member vault state

- **Where:** `VaultCore` keeps member state in seven separate mappings: `sharesOf`,
  `costBasisUsdc`, `lastDepositTime`, `windowCleared`, `skipOptIn`, `queuedExitShares`, and
  `pendingDeposit`, whose struct spans two slots (`uint256` amount, `uint64` time).
- **Change:** `pendingDeposit` as `uint192` amount plus `uint64` time in one slot; shares, deposit
  time and the two flags in one slot; cost basis and queued shares in another.
- **Estimated saving:** about 20,000 on a first deposit, about 40,000 on the first activation (three
  fresh slots become one), about 5,000 on each later deposit or exit. About 65,000 across a new
  member's entry.
- **Risk:** medium. `VaultCore` is 23,227 bytes against the 24,576 EIP-170 limit, and masking code
  costs bytes; the size gate in CI would have to stay green. Public getters can be kept as views.

### 6. Make the reentrancy lock transient

- **Where:** `VaultCore._lock` (`VaultCore.sol:162`), a storage slot flipped 1 to 2 to 1 on every
  guarded call and read by ancestors through `locked()` during look-through.
- **Change:** `TSTORE`/`TLOAD` (EIP-1153). Ethereum has supported it since Dencun and the repo
  already compiles for `cancun`.
- **Estimated saving:** about 4,800 gross per guarded call, about 2,000 net once today's SSTORE
  refund is counted, and about 2,000 per `locked()` read in a look-through.
- **Risk:** low on Ethereum. The invariant stated on `locked()` (`VaultCore.sol:186`) holds unchanged
  within a transaction. Confirm Arc supports EIP-1153 before the same bytecode ships there, since
  the CREATE2 manifest assumes one initcode across chains.

### 7. Cache prices within one exit or rebalance

- **Where:** `_settleExit` (`VaultCore.sol:644`) prices child positions in pass 1, prices every
  basket slice, then recomputes `_childValueWad` for each child in the shortfall loop and prices the
  child deltas again. `executeRebalance` prices both legs of every order through `_valueWad`.
- **Change:** load each basket price into a memory array once per call and pass child values
  forward instead of recomputing them.
- **Estimated saving:** about 3,000 to 6,000 per avoided warm price read. Negligible for a root vault
  exit with no children; tens of thousands for a sub-vault exit with a shortfall, which is where
  `requestExit` hits its 516,831 max.
- **Risk:** low as a refactor, but touches the H-9 and Finding 4/5 code paths, so it deserves the
  same depth of review as those fixes.

### 8. Resolve the parent vault once

- **Where:** `parentVault()` (`VaultCore.sol:529`) reads `_cachedParentVault`, and for a root vault
  in a factory with sub-vaults on it calls `SubVaultRegistry.parentOf` every time, because a root
  vault never caches a nonzero parent. It runs in every `_snapshot` and in every
  `votingEligibleShares` read that `Governance` makes during commit and reveal (`_boundedWeight`,
  `Governance.sol:384`). `propose` reads only the past checkpoints and does not reach it.
- **Change:** pass the parent into the constructor as an immutable (the factory knows it in
  `createChildVault`).
- **Estimated saving:** about 5,000 per member action and per vote with sub-vaults on; about 2,100
  (one cold SLOAD) with sub-vaults off.
- **Risk:** low, but it changes the initcode, and with it the CREATE2 addresses recorded in
  `contracts/config/create2-manifest.json`. Fine for a new v2 deployment; not a patch to live vaults.

### 9. Structural: deploy vaults as clones

- **Where:** `createVault` and `createChildVault` cost about 4.55 million gas, almost all of it the
  23 KB `VaultCore` code deposit (200 gas per byte). At 30 gwei that is about 0.137 ETH per vault,
  paid by the creator.
- **Change:** one `VaultCore` implementation, and an ERC-1167 clone (or a clone with immutable args)
  per vault.
- **Estimated saving:** about 4 million gas per vault created, roughly 0.04 ETH at 10 gwei and 0.12
  ETH at 30 gwei.
- **Cost of doing it:** every call into a vault pays an extra cold `DELEGATECALL` (about 2,600 to
  3,000), and every immutable becomes a storage or code read; on look-through paths that tax is paid
  per child. Break-even is on the order of 1,300 member transactions per vault. It also rewrites
  `VaultDeployer`, the CREATE2 manifest and the EIP-170 strategy, and puts an implementation
  contract in audit scope. Ranked last because it is the largest change, not the smallest saving;
  worth deciding on product grounds (how many vaults, how active) rather than gas alone.
