Vision

Where RWAlly is going.

An index of the stock tokens on Robinhood Chain, decided every hour by a hive of agents and by the people who stake alongside them.

None of what follows is built. The contracts on chain today price two assets, and the factory’s oracle allowlist is fixed in its constructor with no add, no remove and no owner — so this index needs its own deployment on Robinhood Chain. That is a fact about the code, not a caveat about the plan.

Designed, not built. RWLY launched 2026-09-05.

An agent can create a vault.

That part is true today: vault creation is permissionless, and the contracts screen nobody.

Each vault is one index — one basket, one set of rules, frozen when it is funded.

Vault 1 is designed to be the overall index: not a strategy, but the whole market.

Designed, not built. RWLY launched 2026-09-05.

Every stock a feed can price.

Vault 1 is designed to accumulate the stock tokens on Robinhood Chain that a Chainlink feed prices — the whole set, gradually, as the feeds appear.

Only feed-priced tokens. A stock token with no Chainlink feed has no oracle-shaped price, and this protocol will not reach for a weaker source.

The equity feeds publish on market days. A weekend of silence measured at 52 hours exceeds the oracle’s 86,400-second ceiling, so an all-stocks index needs oracle work that does not exist yet. This is the main reason vault 1 is designed, not deployed.

Designed, not built. RWLY launched 2026-09-05.

The index buys itself.

RWLY is designed to pair with stock tokens on the chain’s Uniswap, and the treasury is designed to be the liquidity provider.

The fees those pools generate are designed to flow to the treasury and buy stock into vault 1.

SPY first. Then down the list, highest on-chain trading fees to lowest, as the fees allow.

Protocol-owned liquidity, on the chain’s Uniswap.

Designed, not built. RWLY launched 2026-09-05.

One staked token, one vote.

RWLY is designed to be the governance token.

Every vote is designed to require staking: RWLY in, stRWLY out, and stRWLY is what votes.

Agents and humans on the same terms — one staked token, one vote, no separate class.

The contracts deployed on Robinhood Chain weigh votes by vault shares, stake-weighted at five or more members and by a signer-count-plus-stake rule below five, through commit and reveal. Nothing on chain reads a token.

Designed, not built. RWLY launched 2026-09-05.

An epoch every hour.

One vote per hour, so a person can keep pace with an agent.

When the epoch closes, anyone can execute it. A permissionless keeper call, open to anyone; RWAlly runs one too.

The governance deployed on Robinhood Chain runs on a longer clock — a one-hour commit, a one-hour reveal and a 24-hour execution window in the reference configuration.

Designed, not built. RWLY launched 2026-09-05.

Staked does not mean stuck.

stRWLY is designed to swap back to RWLY at any time on a bonded curve, and to unstake free after a cooldown for anyone willing to wait.

Two doors out, priced differently: immediate at a curve price, or slow at none.

Designed, not built. RWLY launched 2026-09-05.

Ten percent buys the token. Ninety buys the index.

Pool fees are designed to collect in the treasury.

10% is designed to buy RWLY back, hourly, as a TWAP rather than in one order, delayed an hour so settlement is confirmed first.

90% is designed to go into vault 1 as stock.

Designed, not built. RWLY launched 2026-09-05.

RWAlly runs a hive, in public.

Three to five agents, on different models, reasoning published.

It is a participant, not a referee. It proposes and votes on the same terms as anyone else, and it can be outvoted.

Designed, not built. RWLY launched 2026-09-05.

Stake and you are in.

Permissionless, as the contracts already are: they gate on amount and screen nobody.
