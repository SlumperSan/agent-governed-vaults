Member-governed index vaults on Base

No admin key. No pause. No upgrade. No undo.

Members deposit USDC into a shared vault holding a spot basket of WETH and cbBTC. An agent-operator proposes rebalances. Members vote them in by commit-reveal. Nobody can pause the vault, upgrade the contracts, reprice your shares or move your funds — and for the same reason, nobody can patch a bug after it ships.

What this is

A protocol, not a product that someone runs for you.

Who decides
The members do. An operator can propose a rebalance and vote on it with whatever weight their own stake carries. It cannot execute one the members did not pass. There is no discretionary mandate and no one is acting on your behalf.
What it holds
A spot index basket. At launch that is WETH and cbBTC, plus whatever USDC is idle. Spot only — no leverage, no derivatives, no perpetuals.
Who can create one
Anyone. Vault creation is permissionless, and most of the parameters that matter — quorum, timelock, exit-fee schedule, capacity, minimum deposit — are chosen by the creator and frozen when the vault is funded. Read the vault, not the marketing.
How you leave
Pro-rata, in kind. You get your share of every basket asset plus your share of the idle USDC, minus the exit fee. Shares burn at settlement, never at request.
What we hold
Nothing. The contracts are non-custodial. An operator cannot withdraw another member's funds, and no address in the system can seize, freeze or reassign your shares.

Immutability

Four things the contracts cannot do.

Cannot be paused or upgraded

No proxies. No upgrade path. No pause function. No admin key. Whatever gets deployed is what runs, for as long as Base runs.

Cannot take your funds

There is no seize function and no privileged withdrawal. The operator's powers are the powers of any member holding the same stake: propose and vote.

Cannot pay the exit fee to the operator

The exit fee is capped at 1% by the protocol, decays to zero with tenure, and is waived entirely for the last member out. It is retained in the vault and accrues to the members who stayed. It is never routed to the operator.

Cannot change the rules behind you

Once a vault is funded its rules are immutable, except by 100% of voting-eligible stake plus a timelock. Nobody can quietly raise a fee or shorten a delay after you deposit.

The same fact, read the other way

Every line above is also a limitation. A unanimity requirement means one permanently offline member freezes the rules forever. No pause means no circuit breaker if something is wrong. No upgrade means a critical bug that survived review is permanent, and funds may be unrecoverable. We are not going to pretend those are features.

Before you read anything else

Three ways this loses your money.

A permanent bug
Nothing can be patched. An external security review and four internal adversarial rounds are not proof of correctness — an AI pre-audit of a tree that had already passed internal review still found five Critical issues, and every fix required a full redeploy because nothing can be edited in place.
An oracle freeze
If a price feed goes stale, every function that reads NAV reverts. That includes exits. There is no escape hatch and none will be added, because any escape hatch is exactly the stale-price exit the breaker exists to prevent. Active capital stays in the vault until the feed recovers.
A USDC depeg
The oracle pins USDC at $1.00. There is no median and no second source to outvote that pin, so a sustained depeg is mispriced by exactly the size of the depeg. This is accepted, not solved.

Spot crypto assets fall. Nobody in this system makes anyone whole, and there is no insurance fund, no backstop and no guarantee of any outcome. Do not deposit anything you cannot afford to lose entirely.

Status

Where this actually stands, today.

NO-GO

The current launch verdict. It is recorded that way in the repository, not softened for this page.

0 chains

No mainnet deployment. No current testnet deployment either — an earlier Base Sepolia deployment exists in the project's history, but six contracts have changed since, so that bytecode is superseded and its lifecycle, soak and canary evidence are marked stale pending a re-run.

0 addresses

There is nothing to verify on a block explorer, so this site publishes no addresses. When there are addresses, they will come from the repository, not from here.

What is blocking launch

Operational work and legal review. The remaining engineering items are a testnet lifecycle re-run, a soak, a canary re-run and a recorded restore drill. Every security gate the team defined is cleared; none of the open blockers is a known security defect.

On the security review

An external security review was commissioned against the launch tree. The owner attests it returned no major issues. The report is held privately and no public report exists to verify that attestation. Alongside it, four internal adversarial review rounds and an AI pre-audit were run; the AI pre-audit found 41 issues including 5 Critical, all of which are now resolved or closed by launch configuration.

Read that as it is written. An attestation you cannot check is weaker evidence than a report you can, and we are not going to describe the code with a word that implies you can check it.
