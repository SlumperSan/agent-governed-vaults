Fit

For people who already assume the custodian is the risk.

This is built for self-custodying, sovereignty-first people on Base who have been rugged, paused, or frozen out of their own position before, and who now weigh "nobody can pause, upgrade or seize this" above almost everything else. If that describes you, the trade-offs on this site will read as reasonable. If it does not, they will read as unacceptable — and you would be right.

The deal

Governance participation is not a feature. It is the product.

Nobody is running this for you. The operator proposes; you decide. That means the work does not stop at the deposit, and the protocol will not chase you for it.

You have to show up
Voting is commit then reveal, in two phases with deadlines. A commit you never reveal is forfeit and counts as an abstain. Miss the reveal window and your vote simply does not exist — not as a "no", not as anything.
You have to read the vault
Quorum, timelock, exit-fee schedule, minimum deposit, capacity and the commit and reveal durations are all chosen by whoever created the vault and frozen when it is funded. Two vaults built on the same contracts can behave very differently. Nothing on this page tells you what a particular vault's rules are.
You have to accept being outvoted
If a rebalance you dislike passes, your exit queues and settles after the swap executes. You carry the execution you voted against. That is the cost of closing the free option of leaving at pre-vote prices while already knowing the result.
You have to accept a frozen door
If a price feed goes stale, everything that reads NAV reverts — including your exit. There is no escape hatch, and adding one would recreate the exact stale-price exit the breaker exists to prevent. Only capital still inside an un-activated observation window stays reclaimable.

Not for you if

Four groups this is explicitly wrong for at launch.

DAO treasuries and larger allocators

The first vault's capacity cap is 50,000 USDC and a capacity cap is immutable per vault. That cannot absorb a treasury, and it will not be raised — raising it would mean deploying a different vault.

Anyone who wants set-and-forget

There is no autopilot. Skipping votes does not park your position neutrally; it moves quorum, it wastes your commit, and it hands the outcome to whoever did turn up.

Anyone who cannot survive a total loss

Spot crypto assets fall. The code cannot be patched. There is no insurance fund, no backstop, and nobody who makes anyone whole. Deposit only what you can lose entirely.

Anyone in a restricted jurisdiction

Interests in these vaults may be treated as securities or as collective investment scheme interests where you live. Front-end geofencing is intended, but it is a good-faith measure and not a guarantee — the contracts are permissionless and can be called directly by anyone. Take your own advice about your own jurisdiction.

The cap

50,000 USDC is a blast radius, not a queue.

A small first vault is not scarcity and it is not a signal to hurry. It is the largest amount this team is willing to have permanently trapped by a bug that survived review, at a point where nothing can be patched and nothing can be paused. If the code is wrong, the cap is the ceiling on how wrong it can be for anyone.

What that means in practice

There is no allocation to claim, no queue to join, no priority to earn, and nothing on this site to sign up for. If a vault is full, it is full; the answer is a different vault, later, not a place in line.
